NEMM Pathfinder Survival Camp

Developing Future Church Leaders

The Story

We are truly inspired by the incredible individuals we worked with during the Pathfinder Survival Camp and Advanced Survival Camp. It's no surprise they are destined to become leaders in their respective churches.

The NEMM Pathfinder Survival Camp was a transformative experience that brought together young people from across the region, all eager to grow in their faith and develop their leadership skills. Through a series of challenging activities and spiritual sessions, participants discovered their strengths and deepened their commitment to service.

"I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me."

Philippians 4:13

We have gained so much from these amazing people. Kudos to all the youth of Division A Seventh-Day Adventist Youth Department at NEMM. The camp was a powerful reminder that when we step out of our comfort zones, we discover abilities we never knew we had.

Camp Highlights
NEMM Pathfinder Survival Camp — Building Future Leaders

Developing Future Leaders

The survival camp pushed participants beyond their comfort zones, teaching them valuable skills in teamwork, resilience, and faith. These are the leaders who will shape the future of the church and their communities.

Through various challenges — from fire building and shelter construction to navigation and team problem-solving — participants learned that leadership is not about being in charge, but about serving others with humility and courage.

"We have gained so much from this amazing experience — not just in skills and knowledge, but in heart and spirit."

A NEMM Participant
More Moments

A Community of Faith

The NEMM Pathfinder Survival Camp was more than just a training ground — it was a community of faith where young people supported each other, grew together, and deepened their relationship with God. The campfire worship sessions, devotional times, and moments of fellowship created lasting memories and friendships.

As we reflect on this incredible experience, we are reminded that the church's future lies in the hands of these young leaders. Their passion, dedication, and faith give us hope for tomorrow.
