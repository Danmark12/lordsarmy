KSDAES Pathfinder & Adventurer Camp 2025

Four Years of Faithful Service in Kadingilan

The Story

The Lord's ARMY Pathfinder Club proudly facilitated the KSDAES Pathfinder and Adventurer Camp for the fourth consecutive year, marking a significant milestone in the club's ministry. With the theme "Hands to Serve, Hearts to Love," the camp brought together pupils, teachers, and parents who have entrusted the club with this transformative experience.

We extend our sincere gratitude to the pupils, teachers, and parents who continue to believe in us and entrust us with facilitating the KSDAES Pathfinder and Adventurer Camp. Your unwavering support has been instrumental in shaping the club's growth and impact over the past four years.

"Four years of trust have served as our training ground — shaping us to become more effective, resilient, and committed in our ministries."

Lord's ARMY Pathfinder Club

We also express our heartfelt appreciation to the Lord's ARMY Pathfinder Club members who willingly sacrificed their time, work, and studies to make this camp a success. Your dedication, service, and teamwork truly reflect the spirit of ministry and leadership.

Camp Highlights
KSDAES Pathfinder & Adventurer Camp 2025 — Kadingilan, Bukidnon

Opening Night Spectacle

The opening program of KSDAES was nothing short of spectacular — kicked off with a torch parade, fireworks, bonfire, lively songs and yells, and an exciting talents night. It truly highlighted the energy, creativity, and enthusiasm of our Pathfinder and Adventurer campers.

"We have gained so much from this amazing experience — not just in skills and knowledge, but in heart and spirit."

A Lord's ARMY Master Guide

Four Years of Faithful Service

We are delighted to be part of the KSDAES Pathfinder and Adventurer Camp with the theme "Hands to Serve, Hearts to Love." This year marks four years of the Lord's ARMY Pathfinder Club faithfully serving and facilitating Pathfinders in Kadingilan, Bukidnon.

More Moments

Throughout these years, we have witnessed lives transformed, friendships forged, and faith deepened. The camp continues to be a vessel of God's love, reaching out to young hearts and nurturing them to become future leaders.
